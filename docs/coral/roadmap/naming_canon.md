# Coral naming and contract canon

Each final-review finding (FR-01..FR-50) is decided against master v3 ownership (the feature that owns a route, module or contract defines it; consumers follow) and the code at HEAD `2a9cbe2c2`. Edits for specs and sections are in `canon_instructions.json` (exact old and new text, dry-run applied in order). Master edits are applied in `master_v3.json` and logged in its `canon_log` (CANON-01..CANON-20); the adversarial challenge pass is logged in `canon_challenge_log` (CCH-01..CCH-07). E.md, G.md and K.md are not edited; they must be regenerated from master (G.4 consumer lists change for K2, K3, K5 and K7).
